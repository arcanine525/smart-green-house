## Service and grain

[EnergyIntensityService](../../src/greenhouse/queries/intensity.py) composes the
sealed [shared quantity service](../implementation/energy-mart-contract.md) and
sealed harvest adapter on one caller-owned connection and publication ID.
It reads energy quantities independently of harvest dates; there is no raw
Energy×Harvest day join. Each summary has one row/cycle before the drill-across.
ProdA mass is the exact sum of each accepted record times its effective pinned
area; ProdB is disclosed but never enters the denominator. Energy before first
harvest stays in the numerator.

Known distinct/changing areas convert to absolute quantities before summation.
Basis evidence comes only from immutable versions authorized by the chosen
publication's pins. Current enabled flags/latest metadata cannot replace a
frozen selection. Missing/disabled/incompatible basis, incomplete energy,
incomplete harvest and nonpositive/no usable A all keep the ratio NULL and
expose reasons together. Missing values are never zero; accepted zero energy
with positive complete A yields valid0.

Subwindows return descriptive period quantities/ratios when computable, but
`comparison_eligible=false`; they never enter whole-cycle ranking or portfolio.
No claim attributes energy in a harvest week to the fruit harvested that week.
Portfolio requires one publication/version pin, unique cycles, same whole-cycle
window and compatible assumption/status signatures. Mixed windows/versions
produce no comparable eligible population rather than silently averaging.

Public typed DTOs: `HarvestAContribution`, `EnergyIntensity`, `EnergyPortfolio`.
Contract JSONv2 carries exact decimal strings, source revisions/corrections,
source quality/applicability/reasons, effective basis/version/evidence/assumption,
metric`energy-intensity-v1`, quantity`energy-quantity-v1`, window and publication
versions. Wire decoding validates derived ratios and disclosed portfolio sums.
MJ/3.6 and intensity divisions use50 significant digits, half-even; original
MJ is retained unchanged for later BR06. Display rounding never feeds formulas.
